# Competitive analysis

Checked Nov 2026 from public pricing pages and review sites. Competitor feature details beyond what's cited were not verified hands-on; check them in demos before using them in sales.

## The market in one line

Generic "content collection" portals (Content Snare, SuiteDash, file-request tools) are cheap but know nothing about filming. OnlyFans agency CRMs (Infloww, Supercreator, CreatorHero, OnlyMonster, AgencyKey) are built around chatting, fans and revenue. Content *production* (planning what to film, getting the creator to film it well, and approving it) is served badly by both.

## What competitors do better

- **Content Snare**: automatic email/SMS reminders, polished request builder, integrations, long track record (4.9 on Capterra).
- **SuiteDash**: everything in one place (CRM, invoicing, portal) at a flat price, white-label.
- **OF CRMs**: they sit where agency money is made (fan chat, PPV, revenue analytics), so they get budget first. Infloww supports several fan platforms and claims thousands of agencies.

## What they do poorly (our opening)

- None of them helps the creator actually **make** the content: no script on screen, no reference next to the camera, no multi-take filming, no "film along to the sound".
- Generic portals treat a video like any file upload. Agencies still send scripts in WhatsApp, references in DMs, and chase on Telegram.
- OF CRMs' vaults store finished content; they don't manage the request → film → review loop.

## Underserved problems

1. Creators get vague briefs and film the wrong thing → reshoots.
2. Agencies can't see who is late until it's too late.
3. Scripts, references and sounds live in five different apps.
4. Managers can't see each other's work or workload.
5. Content approval is done in chat threads with no record.

## Positioning statement

For creator agencies that run several creators' social and subscription content, [Product] is the production workspace that turns a reference video into a ready-to-film brief and gets it back from the creator, filmed on their phone and ready to approve. Unlike file-request portals and OnlyFans CRMs, it's built around how creators actually film: script on screen, reference beside the camera, and retakes without starting over.

## Partner, don't fight

Agencies already pay for an OF CRM. Position as the production layer that sits next to it, not a replacement. Later: export approved content to the agency's vault or Google Drive.
