## Assumptions (estimates, not measured)

- **Stored per upload = 1.8 x the video's size.** Untouched original clips 1.0, joined copy 0.5, 720p preview 0.1, revisions 0.2. Retakes the creator throws away never leave the phone.
- **Downloaded per upload = 1.3 x size.** Review playback plus one download of the originals.
- **Multipart chunks of 10 MB.**
- **"Upload"** = one delivered video. A 3-clip shot is one upload.

**Upload volume** is new footage per month. **Retained storage** is what accumulates if nothing is ever deleted. The difference is the whole cost story: storage bills grow every month unless files are archived or deleted.

## What the numbers say

1. **Retention matters more than which provider you pick.** At month 12, a 20-creator agency filming 10 videos a day at 100 MB costs $190/month on R2 if nothing is deleted. With originals kept 90 days after approval it costs $48.
2. **Supabase Storage is the most expensive option for this workload.** It costs about 1.7x R2 and 3.5x B2, mostly because of download charges.
3. **B2 is cheapest for storage-heavy video.** Our downloads stay well under 3x what's stored, so they're free. R2 costs about twice as much per TB but has no conditions on downloads and a global upload network.
4. **"Unlimited storage" would be dangerous.** One 50-creator agency at 10 videos a day of 250 MB keeps about 20 TB with 90-day retention. That's ~$300/month on R2 against a $119–$199 plan. Allowances plus add-on blocks are required.
5. **A realistic agency uses much less.** Assume 2 delivered videos per creator per day at ~75 MB, with 90-day retention. That's ~24 GB per creator, about $0.36/month on R2 or $0.17 on B2.
