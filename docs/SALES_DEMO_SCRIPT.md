# Sales demo script (8 minutes)

**Setup before the call**
- Laptop: signed in as the Demo Agency owner (demo.agency@deliveryroom.test).
- Phone: signed in as the creator Desi Destiny. Share the phone screen through the call app, or have it ready to hand over in person.
- Check that the Demo Agency has a section with 2–3 shots, including one dance shot with a reference reel.
- Have one fresh Instagram reel link copied.
- Don't run the demo on hotel or venue Wi-Fi without checking an upload first.

## 0:00 — Open with their problem (1 min)
"How do your managers brief creators right now?" Let them answer. Usually it's WhatsApp, voice notes and Drive.
"And how do you know what's late?" Pause. This is the pain you're solving.

## 1:00 — Brief a shot from a reel (1.5 min)
Laptop, Socials, open the section, add a shot, paste the reel link.
"The video, the sound and the words get pulled in automatically. Add a note, set a due date, assign the creator. Done."
If the import is slow: "It usually takes 10–20 seconds." Move on and come back to it.

## 2:30 — The creator's view (2.5 min) — the moment that sells it
Phone. "This is all she sees. No folders."
Open the shot. Show the reference playing first. Tap **Film it**: the camera opens straight away.
- A talking shot: the teleprompter scrolls the script. Record 2 clips.
- A dance shot: the original sound plays while she films, and continues in the next clip.
Retake clip 2 only. "She doesn't have to redo the whole thing."
Hit **Send**.

## 5:00 — Review (1.5 min)
Laptop: the review queue. The video is there, named by creator and shot.
Approve it. Open another and tap **Request changes** with "lighting too dark".
"She gets that note on the exact shot. No scrolling through chat."

## 6:30 — Team (0.5 min)
Team page: roles (manager, content director) and who's online now.

## 7:00 — Trust (0.5 min)
"Each agency's data is locked to that agency at the database level. Files are private, and links expire. Creators can delete their own account."
Don't say it prevents screenshots. It doesn't, and no app can.

## 7:30 — Close (0.5 min)
"Want to try it with 2 of your creators for 30 days? It's $99, credited if you stay."
Get a date for the onboarding call before you hang up.

## Likely questions
- **Does it post to OnlyFans?** No. OnlyFans has no public API. You get the files organised and approved, then upload as normal.
- **Where's the content stored?** Private storage in Mumbai (Supabase/AWS). Only your agency's staff and the creator can open it.
- **Can creators see each other?** No. Tested.
- **What if a creator leaves?** Remove them; their submitted content stays with the agency.
- **Can we export?** Yes, you can download files. Bulk export is on the roadmap.
- **Price for 30 creators?** Scale, $449/month (Growth covers up to 20).
